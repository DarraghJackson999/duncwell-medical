Before your medical

Anything else, call or WhatsApp 07707 612 400 and we'll sort it in a minute.

What do I need to bring?

Just your glasses, if you wear them for driving. The forms are supplied by the examining doctor and your medical history is obtained beforehand with your consent.

How much does it cost, and how do I pay?

Every driver medical is a flat £100, payable by cash or card on the day. The eyesight examination is part of the appointment, so there is no separate optician visit or fee.

How quickly can I be seen?

There is availability every week and most medicals are completed within 7 days of booking. If your licence can't wait, call us and an urgent appointment can be arranged.

How long does the appointment take?

Around 15–20 minutes. You leave with your completed, signed paperwork ready to submit.

Which licences does the Group 2 (D4) medical cover?

Lorry and bus entitlements: Class 1 and Class 2 HGV, coaches and buses, for first applications and renewals. Farmers and other Group 2 licence holders are welcome too. Not sure which you need? Give us a call.

When is my taxi medical due?

That depends on your licence and your age. Tell us where you are in the process when you book and we'll confirm exactly what you need.
